# Carbon Footprint Tracker

### Introduction
The Carbon Footprint Tracker is a web application designed to help users understand and reduce their carbon emissions. By tracking everyday activities such as transportation, energy consumption, and waste, this tool provides insights into how individual actions contribute to carbon footprints.

Built using Express, React, Langflow, and MongoShell, this app not only calculates your carbon footprint but also offers personalized recommendations to promote sustainable living. Whether you're looking to reduce your environmental impact or simply want to learn more about carbon emissions, this app is here to guide you on your journey toward a greener future.

### Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: React
- **Backend**: Express
- **Database**: MongoDB (MongoShell)
- **AI Integration**: Langflow (for smart recommendations)
- **Environment**: Node.js, MongoDB

### Features
- **Carbon Footprint Calculator:** Calculate your carbon footprint based on various activities such as transportation, energy usage, waste management, etc.
- **Personalized Recommendations:** AI-powered suggestions to help users reduce their carbon emissions.
- **Sustainability Tips:** Actionable insights on eco-friendly practices based on user behavior.
Dashboard: Visual representations (charts and graphs) of your carbon footprint and progress over time.
